# Glossary: shots

Purpose: the owner's own photos after a trip, which recipe each was taken with, how the owner rates them, and which recipes come out best.

| Term | Meaning in this context | Not to be confused with | Name in code |
|---|---|---|---|
| shot | A photo the owner took and uploaded, linked to the recipe it was taken with | recipe image (a photo attached to a recipe, context library) | – (not yet in code) |
| recognition | Finding the recipe a shot was taken with by comparing its EXIF settings with the library; an exact match is assigned automatically, otherwise the owner chooses from the most similar recipes | AI suggestion | – (not yet in code) |
| rating | The owner's score for a shot | favorite (set by hand, context library) | – (not yet in code) |
| ranking | The best recipes of a collection, derived from the ratings of its shots | favorite | – (not yet in code) |
| take over into collection | The one command that hands a shot to the library: the shot becomes a recipe image of its recipe, and the recipe is added to the collection | – | – (not yet in code) |
